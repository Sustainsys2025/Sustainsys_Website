Accounting workflows

Ask Documents

→ Show all bank transactions over £500
📊VAT
VAT Filing
Prepare quarterly VAT returns — identify input and output tax, flag missing VAT numbers, and verify invoice totals before HMRC submission.
→ List all VAT invoices for Q1 2025

📅RECON
Month-End Reconciliation
Cross-reference bank statements with receipts and expense claims. Surface mismatches and unmatched transactions before sending to your accountant.

→ List all Uber and travel receipts
📋CT600
Year-End & Corp Tax
Compile year-end packs for your accountant — disallowed expenses, director costs, immigration visa fees, and P&L summaries for CT600 filing.

Privacy & Security

🖥️
100% On-Device
All LLM inference runs locally via Ollama on your Mac mini M4. No data ever leaves your machine.
🔒
No Cloud APIs
Zero calls to OpenAI, Anthropic, or any external service. Your documents never touch a third-party server.
💾
Local Vector Store
Qdrant runs in local file mode. All 445 vectors are stored in a folder on your machine, not a remote database.
🙈
Gitignored Data
The data_raw/ folder is gitignored by default. Your documents are never committed to version control.
