Why this matters now

The regulatory landscape has changed.

From 2 August 2026, organisations that place or use high-risk AI in the EU face audited obligations on risk management, data governance, technical documentation, human oversight, and post-market monitoring — demonstrable before deployment, regardless of where the organisation is established.

The compliance challenge

Most organisations lack the governance structures, documentation and technical controls the EU AI Act requires. High-risk systems in employment, financial services, education and public administration now need a functioning risk management system, data governance framework, technical documentation, human oversight mechanisms and conformity assessments — all demonstrable before deployment.
