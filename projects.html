AI-Powered Regulatory Reporting

Data Governance & Compliance Automation

An advanced AI platform addressing data governance and regulatory reporting challenges for financial institutions. Automates MiFID II, EMIR, SFTR, and Dodd-Frank (SEC & CFTC) submissions using RAG frameworks to prepare reports and intelligently label trading data.

The Challenge

Financial institutions face mounting pressure from complex regulatory requirements. Managing vast trading databases while ensuring accurate, timely submissions to multiple regulatory bodies is error-prone, time-consuming, and difficult to scale.

Core Solution Features

  • AI-Driven Analysis: LLMs analyze complex trading data structures, identifying relationships and regulatory relevance across massive datasets
  • Intelligent Labeling: RAG-powered classification according to multiple regulatory frameworks
  • Automated Reports: AI agents prepare submission-ready reports with validation and quality checks
  • Regulatory Knowledge: Comprehensive RAG framework with up-to-date regulatory requirements
  • Compliance Validation: Multi-layer validation ensuring data completeness and accuracy

Regulatory Frameworks

  • MiFID II: EU transaction reporting, trade execution data, best execution monitoring
  • EMIR: Derivatives trade reporting, clearing obligations, risk mitigation
  • SFTR: Securities lending and repo transaction reporting
  • Dodd-Frank: US SEC & CFTC compliance, swap data reporting, large trader reporting

Key Benefits

  • Reduce report preparation from weeks to hours, processing millions of records
  • Minimize errors through AI validation, reducing regulatory breach risks
  • Dramatically reduce manual compliance workload and costs
  • Complete audit trails for all transformations and AI decision-making

Technology Stack

RAG Frameworks GPT-4 & Claude Vector Databases Knowledge Graphs Apache Spark NLP & Entity Recognition Regulatory Ontologies Compliance APIs
