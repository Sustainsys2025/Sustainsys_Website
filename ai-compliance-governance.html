Why This Matters Now

The Regulatory Landscape Has Changed

Full enforcement of the EU AI Act begins 2 August 2026 for high-risk AI systems. Organisations that place AI systems on the EU market or use AI outputs within the EU must comply, regardless of where they are established. Penalties reach €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover.

The Compliance Challenge

Most organisations lack the governance structures, documentation, and technical controls the EU AI Act requires. High-risk AI systems used in employment, financial services, education, and public administration now need a functioning risk management system, data governance framework, technical documentation, human oversight mechanisms, and conformity assessments — all demonstrable before deployment.

Our Approach

We help you build an AI compliance programme that works in practice, not just on paper. We start with a complete inventory of your AI systems, classify each by risk level, map applicable obligations under the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and NIST AI RMF, then design and implement the controls, documentation, and monitoring your organisation needs to demonstrate ongoing compliance.

What We Deliver

Comprehensive AI Regulatory Compliance

We help organisations assess, design, and operationalise compliance for AI systems across the full lifecycle — from concept through deployment and ongoing operations.

Regulatory Impact Assessment

We deliver comprehensive regulatory impact assessments for AI initiatives to identify applicable legal, standards, and governance obligations early in the lifecycle. We map those obligations into actionable controls covering risk management, technical documentation, transparency, human oversight, data governance, accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity — so your team can move from uncertainty to a clear compliance roadmap.

Production Safeguards Review

We assess the sufficiency of your production safeguards, including policy controls, approval workflows, monitoring, logging, incident response, escalation paths, and evidence retention. This ensures your AI systems are not only designed for compliance, but also operated with the guardrails needed for ongoing regulatory and reputational risk reduction.

Conformity Assessment Support

High-risk AI systems require conformity assessments before market placement. We prepare your systems for internal control-based assessments and, where required, notified body assessments — including EU declaration of conformity, CE marking readiness, and registration in the EU AI database as mandated under the EU AI Act.

Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment

Article 27 of the EU AI Act mandates deployers of high-risk AI systems to conduct Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments (FRIAs) before deployment. We conduct these assessments, examining algorithmic bias, privacy risks, discriminatory outcomes, and broader societal implications of your AI systems against fundamental rights standards.

Compliance Lifecycle

From Assessment to Continuous Monitoring

Our engagement follows the AI compliance lifecycle — aligning with the NIST AI RMF's Govern, Map, Measure, Manage functions and the ISO/IEC 42001 Plan-Do-Check-Act methodology.

Phase 01 — Assess

Discovery & Classification

Build a complete AI system inventory, classify each system by risk level (prohibited, high-risk, limited, minimal), identify applicable regulations, and document the rationale for each classification. Includes regulatory scoping and applicability analysis across all relevant jurisdictions.

Phase 02 — Design

Framework & Control Design

Map regulatory obligations to actionable controls. Design your AI governance framework including organisational policies, roles and responsibilities, risk tolerance thresholds, and accountability structures. Draft the technical documentation and model cards required for conformity assessment.

Phase 03 — Implement

Controls & Safeguards

Implement human oversight mechanisms, logging and monitoring infrastructure, approval workflows, incident response procedures, and escalation paths. Prepare evidence packs and compliance documentation. Conduct bias testing, model validation, and Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments.

Phase 04 — Monitor

Ongoing Compliance

Continuous post-deployment monitoring, drift detection, and periodic compliance checks. Maintain audit readiness with evidence retention and regulator response support. Track evolving regulatory requirements and update your controls as frameworks mature.
