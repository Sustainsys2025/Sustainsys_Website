AI-Powered Regulatory Reporting

Data Governance & Compliance Automation
We are developing an advanced AI-powered platform to address the complex data governance and regulatory reporting challenges faced by financial institutions. Working with large-scale banking databases, our solution automates MiFID II, EMIR, SFTR, and Dodd-Frank (SEC & CFTC) submissions using cutting-edge RAG frameworks to prepare reports and intelligently label trading data.

The Challenge

Financial institutions face mounting pressure from increasingly complex regulatory requirements. Managing vast trading databases while ensuring accurate, timely submissions to multiple regulatory bodies presents significant operational challenges. Traditional manual processes are error-prone, time-consuming, and struggle to scale.

Core Solution Features

  • 🧠 AI-Driven Analysis: Advanced LLMs analyze complex trading data structures, identifying relationships and regulatory relevance across massive datasets
  • 🏷️ Intelligent Labeling: RAG-powered classification and labeling according to multiple regulatory frameworks
  • 📋 Automated Reports: AI agents autonomously prepare submission-ready reports with validation and quality checks
  • 🔍 Regulatory Knowledge: Comprehensive RAG framework with up-to-date regulatory requirements
  • ✓ Compliance Validation: Multi-layer validation ensuring data completeness and accuracy

Regulatory Frameworks

  • 📊 MiFID II: EU transaction reporting, trade execution data, best execution monitoring
  • 📉 EMIR: Derivatives trade reporting, clearing obligations, risk mitigation
  • 📑 SFTR: Securities lending and repo transaction reporting
  • 🇺🇸 Dodd-Frank: US SEC & CFTC compliance, swap data reporting, large trader reporting

Key Benefits

  • ⚡ Reduce report preparation from weeks to hours, processing millions of records
  • 🎯 Minimize errors through AI validation, reducing regulatory breach risks
  • 💰 Dramatically reduce manual compliance workload and associated costs
  • 📊 Complete audit trails for all transformations and AI decision-making
