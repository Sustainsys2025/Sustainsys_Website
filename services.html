AccountIQ — New Product

Your Accounts, On Your Machine

AccountIQ is an AI accounting assistant that runs entirely on your device. Your invoices, bank statements, and tax records never leave your computer — not to a cloud server, not to an API, nowhere. That's not a feature, it's the whole point.

It handles the work that eats up your week: VAT returns, bank reconciliation, year-end packs for your accountant. Upload your documents, ask questions in plain English, and get answers with sources. Built specifically for UK businesses and their accountants.
