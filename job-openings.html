AI Automation Engineer

📍 Remote / Hybrid (Cambridge, UK)
💼 Mid to Senior Level
⏰ Full-Time
Open

We're looking for an AI Automation Engineer to design, build and maintain intelligent automation systems that streamline enterprise workflows. You'll work at the intersection of AI/ML and process automation — building agentic pipelines, integrating LLMs into production workflows, and developing self-healing automation frameworks that reduce manual effort across regulated industries.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and implement end-to-end AI-powered automation pipelines for enterprise clients
  • Build agentic workflows using LLM orchestration frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, or similar)
  • Develop intelligent document processing systems using OCR, NLP and classification models
  • Create self-healing automation scripts that adapt to changing data formats and system states
  • Integrate AI models with RPA tools and existing enterprise systems (APIs, databases, cloud services)
  • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated testing and deployment of AI workflows
  • Monitor automation performance, identify bottlenecks, and optimise throughput and accuracy
  • Collaborate with product, data science and compliance teams to translate business requirements into automated solutions
  • Implement guardrails, logging and audit trails for AI-driven automation in regulated environments
  • Document automation architectures, runbooks and operational procedures

Required Skills & Experience

  • 3+ years of experience in automation engineering, AI/ML engineering, or a related role
  • Strong programming skills in Python; experience with async patterns and task orchestration
  • Hands-on experience with LLMs (GPT, Claude, LLaMA) and prompt engineering for production use cases
  • Familiarity with agentic AI frameworks and multi-step reasoning pipelines
  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and serverless architectures
  • Working knowledge of containerisation (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes)
  • Experience with REST APIs, webhooks, and event-driven architectures
  • Understanding of data pipelines and ETL processes
  • Strong problem-solving skills and ability to debug complex distributed systems
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to work in cross-functional teams

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines and vector databases
  • Knowledge of RPA platforms (UiPath, Automation Anywhere, or Power Automate)
  • Familiarity with regulatory compliance frameworks (MiFID II, EMIR, SOC 2)
  • Experience with MLOps tools (MLflow, Weights & Biases, or similar)
  • Understanding of AI safety, evaluation frameworks, and model governance
  • Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform, Pulumi)
  • Knowledge of message queues and streaming platforms (Kafka, RabbitMQ, SQS)
  • Contributions to open-source automation or AI projects
  • Degree in Computer Science, AI, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary (commensurate with experience)
  • Flexible remote-first working with optional hybrid access to our Cambridge office
  • Work on production AI systems used by real enterprise clients
  • Professional development budget for courses, certifications, and conferences
  • Access to cutting-edge AI tools, cloud resources, and GPU compute
  • Collaborative team of AI engineers, data scientists, and domain experts
  • Health and wellness benefits
  • Opportunity to shape the future of AI-driven automation in regulated industries
