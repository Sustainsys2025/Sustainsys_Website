AI-Powered Regulatory Reporting

Data Governance & Compliance Automation
We are developing an advanced AI-powered platform to address the complex data governance and regulatory reporting challenges faced by financial institutions. Working with large-scale banking databases, our solution tackles the intricate requirements of major regulatory frameworks including MiFID II, EMIR, SFTR, and Dodd-Frank (SEC & CFTC), utilizing cutting-edge RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) frameworks to automate submission report preparation and intelligent trading data labeling.

The Challenge

Financial institutions face mounting pressure from increasingly complex regulatory requirements. Managing vast trading databases while ensuring accurate, timely submissions to multiple regulatory bodies (MiFID II for EU markets, EMIR for derivatives, SFTR for securities financing, and Dodd-Frank for US markets) presents significant operational and compliance challenges. Traditional manual processes are error-prone, time-consuming, and struggle to scale with the volume and complexity of modern trading data.

Our Solution

🧠
AI-Driven Data Understanding
Advanced LLMs analyze and comprehend complex trading data structures, automatically identifying data relationships, patterns, and regulatory relevance across massive datasets.
🏷️
Intelligent Data Labeling
RAG-powered classification and labeling of trading data according to multiple regulatory frameworks, ensuring accurate categorization and compliance mapping.
📋
Automated Report Generation
AI agents autonomously prepare submission-ready reports for MiFID II, EMIR, SFTR, and Dodd-Frank, including validation and quality checks.
🔍
Regulatory Knowledge Base
Comprehensive RAG framework incorporating up-to-date regulatory requirements, enabling context-aware interpretation of complex compliance rules.
✓
Compliance Validation
Multi-layer validation ensuring data completeness, accuracy, and adherence to specific regulatory submission standards before report finalization.
📈
Scalable Architecture
Enterprise-grade infrastructure handling millions of trading records with distributed processing and real-time data governance capabilities.

Regulatory Frameworks Supported

  • 📊 MiFID II (Markets in Financial Instruments Directive)
    Comprehensive transaction reporting for EU markets, including detailed trade execution data, best execution monitoring, and systematic internaliser reporting requirements.
  • 📉 EMIR (European Market Infrastructure Regulation)
    Derivatives trade reporting to trade repositories, covering OTC derivatives, clearing obligations, and risk mitigation techniques for uncleared derivatives.
  • 📑 SFTR (Securities Financing Transactions Regulation)
    Securities lending and repo transaction reporting, providing transparency in securities financing markets and shadow banking activities.
  • 🇺🇸 Dodd-Frank Act - SEC & CFTC
    US regulatory compliance for securities (SEC) and derivatives (CFTC) including swap data reporting, large trader reporting, and consolidated audit trail (CAT) requirements.

Key Benefits

  • ⚡ Rapid Processing
    Reduce report preparation time from weeks to hours, processing millions of records with consistent accuracy and meeting tight regulatory deadlines.
  • 🎯 Enhanced Accuracy
    Minimize human error through AI-powered validation and cross-reference checking, significantly reducing regulatory breach risks and penalties.
  • 💰 Cost Reduction
    Dramatically reduce manual compliance workload, freeing compliance teams to focus on strategic risk management and regulatory advisory.
  • 🔄 Continuous Adaptation
    RAG framework automatically incorporates regulatory updates, ensuring ongoing compliance as rules evolve without manual reconfiguration.
  • 📊 Audit Trail & Transparency
    Complete traceability of all data transformations and AI decision-making processes, providing clear audit trails for regulatory examinations.
