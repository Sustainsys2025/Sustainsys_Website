Regulatory Business Analyst

📍 Remote / Hybrid
💼 Mid to Senior Level
⏰ Full-Time
Open Position

Join our regulatory compliance team as a Techno-Functional Business Analyst specializing in MiFID II and EMIR reporting. You'll bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation, working on cutting-edge AI-powered regulatory reporting solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Analyze and document regulatory requirements for MiFID II and EMIR reporting frameworks
  • Act as a bridge between business stakeholders and technical development teams
  • Define functional and technical specifications for regulatory reporting solutions
  • Map trading data to regulatory reporting requirements and schemas
  • Conduct gap analysis between existing systems and regulatory obligations
  • Support UAT activities and validation of regulatory reports
  • Collaborate with data teams to ensure data quality and completeness for submissions
  • Stay updated on regulatory changes and assess impact on current implementations
  • Document business processes, data flows, and system integrations

Required Skills & Experience

  • Proven experience with MiFID II transaction reporting requirements
  • Strong understanding of EMIR trade reporting and derivatives regulations
  • Experience in financial services, particularly capital markets or trading operations
  • Ability to understand and analyze complex regulatory documentation
  • Strong data mapping and data lineage documentation skills
  • Experience working with regulatory reporting systems and platforms
  • Understanding of trading lifecycle and financial instruments
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities
  • Strong communication skills to interact with both business and technical stakeholders
  • Experience with SQL and data query tools

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of SFTR (Securities Financing Transactions Regulation)
  • Familiarity with Dodd-Frank reporting requirements (SEC & CFTC)
  • Experience with ARM (Approved Reporting Mechanism) submissions
  • Understanding of ISO 20022 messaging standards
  • Experience with AI/ML-based regulatory reporting solutions
  • Knowledge of data governance and data quality frameworks
  • Familiarity with regulatory ontologies and taxonomies
  • Experience with JIRA, Confluence, or similar collaboration tools
  • Certifications in business analysis (e.g., CBAP, CCBA)

What We Offer

  • Opportunity to work with cutting-edge AI-powered regulatory solutions
  • Competitive salary and benefits package
  • Flexible working arrangements
  • Professional development and training opportunities
  • Exposure to multiple regulatory frameworks and jurisdictions
  • Collaborative team environment with industry experts
