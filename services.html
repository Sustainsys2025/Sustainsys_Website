Our Services

We architect and deliver enterprise-grade AI solutions spanning the full lifecycle — from Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, multi-model orchestration frameworks, and high-throughput vector database architectures to production-hardened agentic systems, autonomous reasoning engines, and intelligent code companions purpose-built for mission-critical workloads.

Our dedicated AI Safety & Security practice embeds compliance-by-design into every deployment, enforcing rigorous governance protocols, adversarial resilience testing, prompt-injection defence layers, and alignment with evolving regulatory mandates including the EU AI Act and GDPR.

Backed by deep-domain MLOps & DevOps expertise, end-to-end CI/CD automation, and ITIL-aligned managed support services, we operationalise AI at scale — ensuring observability, model drift monitoring, and continuous delivery across heterogeneous infrastructure.

Our Cloud Transformation consultants accelerate migration and modernisation across AWS, Azure, GCP, and hybrid environments, while our specialist Murex consulting team delivers end-to-end capital-markets platform upgrades, FRTB, IBOR transition, and XVA analytics.

Through our AI Academy, we upskill your teams with immersive, hands-on training in prompt engineering, LLM fine-tuning, responsible AI governance, and agentic workflow design — empowering organisations to build, secure, and sustain intelligent systems with confidence.

AccountIQ — New Product

AI-Powered Accounting Software

Built by SustainSys, AccountIQ is a privacy-first, on-device accounting assistant powered by local LLM inference. It processes invoices, bank statements, receipts and spreadsheets entirely on your machine — no data ever leaves your device.

From VAT filing and month-end reconciliation to year-end corporation tax preparation, AccountIQ automates the workflows that matter most to UK businesses and their accountants.
