Cloud Transformations

Our cloud services range from the assessment & discovery to the implementation, migration and support. We are equally equipped in AWS, Azure, GCP, Oracle & Alibaba or on-premises private cloud.

Our Key Cloud services include:

  • Cloud management & optimisation
  • Cloud engineering and automation
  • Cloud security
  • Cloud strategy
  • Cloud migration
  • Data transformation

We are key advocates of supporting multi and hybrid cloud strategy and tirelessly work to stay up-to-date with the ever-changing landscape in this space.

Murex Expertise

Consultancy and resourcing services for Murex 3.1 for cloud & data migrations, upgrades, enhancements and support.

Our focus is to help clients notch up their ROI on Murex. We have helped clients in the areas of Murex upgrades, migration from Mx2.11 to Mx3.1, integrating their trading and risk systems in Murex workflows, and delivering datamart reporting solutions.

We have contributed immense value to client efforts to optimize costs by overhauling the reporting and risk infrastructures of Mx implementations. In recent engagements, we have successfully contributed towards the implementations of regulatory projects such as FRTB, IBOR transitions, XVA and ISDA SIMM.
